## Which Roblox games use a "seen = safe / unseen = danger" mechanic or a close relative, and how are they doing?

### Takeaway
Four families exist on Roblox, all built on the **player's own gaze or a light source**, never on a fixed security camera's field of view: (1) light as a safe zone (Lights Out, Stay In The Light, Nightlight, 99 Nights' campfire); (2) "it only moves when nobody is looking" (PERIPHERAL, DON'T BLINK, Don't Blink! [Alpha], DOORS' Monument, DON'T LOOK BACK's Effigy, SCP-173 demos); (3) cameras as a tool for watching or recording (ANALOG, Still Watching, Anomaly Detected, Observation Duty); (4) the reverse, where being seen kills you (Hide from Camera or Die, Look Away!, Bob is Watching, Camera Tag). The healthiest close relative is **PERIPHERAL (BETA)**, about 700 CCU, 81.8 % likes, released Sep 28, 2026. Most pure "don't blink" and "stay in the light" titles are small or close to dead.

### Inferences
- The "light safe zones that rotate on a timer" idea (Lights Out Classic: "Every 30 seconds, the lights around the map will change") is the closest existing precedent for BLIND SPOT's LIVE → FLICKER → BLACKOUT cycle, but it has been retired in favour of a battery-collecting chapter format.
- The "observation stops the monster" idea is already proven in a large game (DOORS' Monument, including the "one watches while the others work" co-op pattern that BLIND SPOT's camcorder formalises), and the newest game to make it central (PERIPHERAL) is pulling 700 to 1,500 CCU one month after release. That points to demand for the idea, not a saturated niche.
- Pure "don't blink" games (DON'T BLINK, Don't Blink! [Alpha], the SCP-173 demos) either died at 0 CCU or survive on legacy SCP traffic. Without a loop around it (objectives, rounds, progression), the gaze mechanic alone doesn't seem to hold players.
- "Hide from Camera or Die" shows that a camera-as-threat party game can spike (5.6K peak, 8.5M visits in two months) but rates poorly (55 %) and falls fast (−40 % weekly). The camera framing draws clicks; BLIND SPOT's inversion (camera = safety) is unclaimed.

## Is any game a direct match (being on camera = safe from a monster that lives in unwatched space)?

### Takeaway
**No.** None of the games checked makes a camera's field of view (security camera, CCTV or camcorder) the thing that keeps players alive. The closest are **PERIPHERAL (BETA)** (the creature retreats when you look at it, light is your ally, flashlight battery, power phases), **Lights Out Classic** (rotating light safe zones every 30 s, now archived), **DON'T BLINK** (an asymmetric 1v/2v-all "monster moves when not watched" PvP, now dead) and **DOORS' Monument** (one player watches while the others work). The reverse, **Hide from Camera or Die**, is the only camera-framed survival game with real traffic.

### Inferences
- BLIND SPOT's specific pieces don't appear in any game checked: fixed cameras whose fields of view are the safe zones, REC lights that show which cameras survive the blackout, a battery camcorder that protects a *teammate*, dead players running a security camera, and a PvP Unseen who only sees blind spots and cuts camera cables. The general idea ("observation stops the monster", "light protects") is well-known on Roblox, so the hook needs to be the camera framing and the co-op roles, not "monster that moves when unseen".
- PERIPHERAL is the most direct competitor for the same audience (co-op horror, the gaze holds off one creature, light and battery management, a phase change when the power comes back) and is currently growing. It is worth a hands-on look.
